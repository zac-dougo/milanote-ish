# Visual planning

Personal storyboarding and collecting images and text on boards.

## Language

**Card**:
An item placed on a board and positioned freely by the user. A card can combine different kinds of content, such as a link and an image, along with a heading and supporting text.

**Content block**:
A piece of content within a card, such as a heading, image, text, quote, link, checklist, or file. Blocks form a stack and can be reordered within the card.

**Image card**:
A card whose main content is an image, with an optional heading and supporting text on the same card.

**Quote card**:
A card whose main content is quoted text, with an optional heading and supporting text on the same card.

**Local library**:
The collection of boards and their saved content stored on the user's Mac.

**Board**:
A space for collecting and arranging images, text, and nested boards.

**Main board**:
A top-level board, outside any other board.

**Home**:
The library's root canvas, containing main-board tiles.

**Board tile**:
The item representing a board in its parent canvas. Opening the tile navigates to that board.

**Nested board**:
A board contained within another board.

**Connector**:
A line or arrow between cards or board tiles that expresses a relationship or sequence.
